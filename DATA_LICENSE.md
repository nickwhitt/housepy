## A note on underlying facts

Historical facts themselves (birth dates, successions, etc.) are not subject
to copyright. This license covers HousePy's specific compilation, structuring,
and presentation of that information as a dataset — not the underlying
historical record, which remains freely available from primary and
secondary sources.
